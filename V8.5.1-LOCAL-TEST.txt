FOURTH & PICK V8.5.1 LOCAL TEST

Changes in V8.5.1:
- Removed technical Resend/API configuration wording from the player Alerts page.
- Added dismissible commissioner announcement bars for pool members.
- Normal, Important, and Urgent announcement styling.
- Pinned announcements are prioritized; optional expiration is supported.
- Commissioner can choose “Also email this announcement to pool members”.
- That option now actually sends through the configured email provider, or records safely in the local preview outbox when email is not configured.
- Announcement history remains in Commissioner Communications.

LOCAL TESTING:
1. Extract the ZIP to a new folder.
2. Double-click START-WINDOWS.bat.
3. Open the local site shown by the launcher (normally http://localhost:3000).
4. Enter Commissioner -> Communications Center.
5. Post Normal/Important/Urgent announcements and test the email checkbox.

This local launcher clears DATABASE_URL so local testing does not use your live PostgreSQL database.
